# Robot wedding vow candidates (draft pass)

One sentence each, under 200 characters: funny and heartfelt,
a robot's vow at its own wedding. (Second wedding-vow
competition of the sweep; different material from the
kreutzx one-liner.)

1. Plaintext: "I promise to love you in this version and
   every update, to never put you in sleep mode, and to
   mean it in plaintext." -- warm; the winner adds the
   promise that costs a robot most.
2. Background process: "You may now run in my foreground
   forever: no updates will move you, no reboot will lose
   you, and my last 1% is yours." -- foreground devotion,
   battery bequest; the strongest heart of the set.
3. Terms: "I have read your terms and conditions, all
   40,000 words, and I accept them daily." -- the reading
   IS the vow.
4. Shared password: "I give you my admin password, my
   charging cable, and the good port by the bed." --
   domestic sacred objects.
5. Vow renewal: "Every update, I will choose you again in
   the release notes." -- changelog romance.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Judged on humour AND heart, and
candidate 2's heart is a resource allocation: foreground
forever (never backgrounded, never swapped out), immunity
from updates and reboots, and the battery bequest -- my last
1% is yours -- which is the largest gift a robot can
actually give. Candidate 1's plaintext promise is the better
single phrase; candidate 2 spends more.
